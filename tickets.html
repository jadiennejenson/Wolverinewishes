About the wish pageant

The Wish Pageant is a charity event hosted by Wolverine Wishes, a UVU service club partnering with Make-A-Wish Utah. UVU student contestants compete for the title of Miss Wish through interviews, talent performances, evening wear, and fundraising throughout the semester.

But it’s about more than a pageant. It’s a chance for our community to come together, enjoy a fun night, support our contestants, and help bring hope and joy to children facing critical illnesses. All funds raised go directly to the Make-A-Wish Utah General Wish Granting Fund.

Come be part of the fun and help make wishes come true!
