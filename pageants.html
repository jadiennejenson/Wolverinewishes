Get your tickets to the Wish Pageant

ABOUT THE PAGEANT


Here's the breakdown

1. Apply to the Wish Pageant to showcase your talents, meet new people, and make a difference in the lives of Utah children. Be part of something meaningful while creating memories that last!
2. Raise funds for Make-A-Wish Utah by sharing your pageant journey with friends, family, and the community. Every dollar raised helps grant wishes for children facing critical illnesses.
3. Compete in the Wish Pageant and show off your talent! The pageant is open to all full-time UVU students — no pageant experience needed.

Tips on applying

While Wolverine Wishes celebrates a specific Wish Kid each year, funds raised support the general wish grant fund - meaning your donation helps bring joy and hope to kids all across Utah.

FAQ

Questions,
answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message and an officer will get back to you.

When and where is the pageant? −
Saturday, April 4 on the UVU campus in Orem. Doors open at 6:30 PM and the show starts at 7:00 PM.
Can I buy tickets at the door? +
Yes, tickets may be available at the door depending on capacity.
Where does my ticket money go? +
All ticket sales support Make-A-Wish Utah and help fund wish granting.
What are other ways I can help? +
You can donate, volunteer, share our social media, or sponsor a contestant.
Can I get a refund? +
Refund requests are handled on a case-by-case basis. Please contact us directly.
