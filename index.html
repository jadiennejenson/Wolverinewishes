What is the Wolverine Wishes Fundraiser?

Each spring, Wolverine Wishes brings the UVU community together for a campus-wide fundraiser supporting Make-A-Wish Utah. Through events like the Wish Pageant, students raise money to help make wishes come true. While we highlight one Wish Kid each year, all funds support the general wish fund, bringing hope and joy to children across Utah.

About

Through Wishmakers on Campus, college students rally together to plan and coordinate fundraising campaigns to help Make-A-Wish® grant more wishes to kids with critical illnesses. Utilizing the Wish Your Way fundraising program, participants can register at no cost and have access to utilize online fundraising tools to collect donations, create teams for friendly competitions between sports teams, grade levels and more, allowing quick activation and a variety of collection opportunities. Whether your events are happening during the school day or after school at a sporting event, your students have access to fundraising tools every step of the way. Students who utilize both online fundraising and in-person event collections (bake sales, car washes, penny wars, and more) raise more funds on average. The Wish Your Way fundraising app is excellent for communicate with each other and for fundraisers to have collection opportunities at their fingertips.

Wishmakers on Campus is how Wolverine Wishes, The Wish Pageant, Wish Week, and Make-A-Wish March are made possible. It allows for smoother fundraising and more support from Make-A-Wish Utah. When we participate in Wishmakers on Campus, remember, all funds raised go towards the Make-A-Wish Utah General Wish Granting Fund (often times shorted to "grant local wish kids wishes"). We are not responsible for our Wish Kid's wish. Our Wish Kid can be involved in the campaign and their story can be shared, but we are not giving the money to solely their wish.

Donate

Wish Your Way is how schools, organizations, and individuals fundraise for Make-A-Wish. Once you have a campaign on the site, you will have a log in and your own URL. This is where people donate to Make-A-Wish Utah through your campaign, that way you can tally how much you raised but not have to interact with the money.

Pageants

The Wish Pageant is a fundraiser for Make-A-Wish Utah. It is a pageant that is open to all students at Utah Valley University. The contestants are judged on their talent, interview, and overall presentation. The contestants are also required to raise money for Make-A-Wish Utah. The contestant who raises the most money is crowned the winner of the pageant and will be the face of the campaign for the year.

Tickets

Ticketing is done through Campus Connection. They make it a smooth, easy process so that you don't have to stress over it. Their fees are covered by the university. The ticketing site it hosted on university ticketing. You decide how much tickets are when you submit the scheduling request form through clubs. There is a $3 transaction fee that the site gets and the guest pays at check out. Tickets are sold online and at the door.
