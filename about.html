OUR STORY

From a service project to a campus tradition

Wolverine Wishes started in 2024 as a service project within the Wolverine Ambassadors, UVU's student leadership program. After connecting with Make-A-Wish Utah, the team joined the Student WishMakers program, becoming one of the only college groups in Utah to take part.

When funding for the project was cut, the founders turned it into an official UVU club. Today, Wolverine Wishes is run by a student executive board and hosts the annual Wish Pageant, our biggest fundraiser of the year.

OUR JOURNEY

Three years, three Wish Kids

Each spring, our campaign is built around one local Wish Kid.

2024 · $4,500 raised

Quinn

A Star Wars and soccer fan, Quinn played a match with the UVU men's soccer team and trained to become a Jedi, then revealed his wish to battle Darth Vader at Disney World.

2026 · $13,000 raised

Jade

Jade loved princesses and ponies. The UVU Rodeo team hosted a rodeo just for her, and our club surprised her with a princess party at UVU's art museum.

2026 · $2,500 raised

Emmy

At our first Wish Pageant, Emmy served as a judge, sang during the talent portion, and was crowned the first-ever Miss Wish Jr.
