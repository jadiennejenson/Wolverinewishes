What is the Wolverine Wishes Fundraiser?

Each spring, Wolverine Wishes brings the UVU community together for a campus-wide fundraiser supporting Make-A-Wish Utah. Through events like the Wish Pageant, students raise money to help make wishes come true. While we highlight one Wish Kid each year, all funds, support the general wish fund, bring hope and joy to children across Utah.

While Wolverine Wishes celebrates a specific Wish Kid each year, funds raised support the general wish grant fund - meaning your donation helps bring joy and hope to kids all across Utah.
Apply to the Wish Pageant to showcase your talents, meet new people, and make a difference in the lives of Utah children. Be part of something meaningful while creating memories that last!
Your ticket helps make wishes come true! Join us for the Wish Pageant and support children facing critical illnesses. All funds raised go towards Make-A-Wish Utah's General Wish Granting Fund. Thank you for making a difference!
