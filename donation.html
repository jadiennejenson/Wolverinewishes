What your donation helps with

The Wish Pageant is the heart of Wolverine Wishes' annual fundraiser in partnership with Make-A-Wish Utah. This inclusive, co-ed competition invites full-time UVU students to showcase their talents, creativity, and compassion — all while raising funds to grant life-changing wishes for Utah children facing critical illnesses.




1

Funding real wishes

Your gift goes straight to Make-A-Wish Utah's General Wish Granting Fund, which grants wishes for kids facing critical illnesses all across the state.

2

Giving kids something to look forward to

A wish can bring hope, strength, and joy to a child and their family during one of the hardest times of their lives.

3

Supporting every Wish Kid

While Wolverine Wishes celebrates a specific Wish Kid each year, funds raised support the general wish grant fund — meaning your donation helps bring joy and hope to kids all across Utah.

Every gift brings a child closer to their wish.

All funds go to Make-A-Wish Utah's General Wish Granting Fund.
